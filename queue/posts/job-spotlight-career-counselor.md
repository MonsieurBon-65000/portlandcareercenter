Do you have an absolute love of humanity and the sometimes messy work of career and personal development?

Do you worry about people conducting fruitless job searches?  Does it keep you up at night?

![fishermen-silhouette-sunset-39313.jpeg](/images/fishermen-silhouette-sunset-39313.jpeg)Would you rather teach people how to fish than give them a fish?

These are a few of the requirements needed to be a career counselor.  Of course skillset is important as well.

According to Onet Online, [https://www.onetonline.org/](https://www.onetonline.org/) among the skills needed are Active Listening, Social Perceptiveness, Critical Thinking and Assessing. Important values include the desire to help and empower others, identifying the best in people and problem solving.

While this is a social career, many career consultants have their own private practice.  Some work for organizations. You need to enjoy working socially with others and working alone.

Most career counselors succeed by acting with high trust and assuming positive intent. They may be passionate about the resume, networking, interviewing, branding or helping people identify their career and calling. Career counselors can specialize but also be generalists. They can assume the role of mentor, teacher, resource manager, coach and social worker. ![pexels-photo-669013.jpeg](/images/pexels-photo-669013.jpeg)Currently this career has a bright outlook.  Industries that hire career counselors include:  

  
-   Educational Services
  
-   Health Care
  
-   Outplacement
  
-   Counseling Centers
  
-   Non Profits.
  

  
Know too, that there are other similar titles that might be a fit for you if you are considering this career.  Vocational Counselor, Guidance Counselor, Career Center Director, and Academic Advisor to name but a few.

When I was deciding what to be when I grew up, I met with a career consultant.  She helped me narrow down my choice to teaching, counseling and writing.  As a career counselor, I do all three of my top skills every day.

If the above fits you, know that you have a lifetime of contributing to the common good and enjoying your work life in ways that other careers do not.  

_Act as if what you do makes a difference. It does._
