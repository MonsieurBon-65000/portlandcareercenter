By Aly Anliker

Here are seven of my recommended practices to follow when you find yourself in a job or career transition. ![keyboard-417090\_960\_720](/images/keyboard-417090_960_720.jpg)  

  
2.  **Keep your resume updated**.
  

  
This is important for a variety of reasons.  You never know when a great opportunity might come your way.  And you never know when your organization might restructure and decide that your position or department will be eliminated.

## 2\. Create an accomplishment based resume

In addition to the above, you want your resume to be a history of your accomplishments for the last ten to fifteen years.  Keep track of what you have done, the actions you took and the results of the actions you took by created an accomplishment bullet to add to your resume. This is easier after a performance review when you have records you can refer to.

## 3\. Create a presence on LinkedIn and maintain that presence

LinkedIn is the number one social media site related to jobs and careers.  Not only do you want to create a powerful profile, I recommend you use the site to stay in touch with your network.  Every so often share a post that your network will find helpful.  This helps you nurture your brand, what you want to be known for, which in turn helps you stay employable. Consider other social media sites.

![social-media-1795578\_\_340](/images/social-media-1795578__340.jpg)

## 4\. Don’t spend a lot of time on the cover letter

Of all the job search activities you will engage in, spend the least amount of time on your cover letter.  Create a good template and change that template to match the position. When I’ve interviewed HR professionals and hiring managers, about half say they read the cover letter.  Do make sure to have someone proof your spelling and grammar, however.

## 5\. Plan to devote at least 30 hours a week to your job search

Run your search like a job.  Only now your job is to find a job.  Do not look for work on weekends unless it’s a networking event, because you will want to recharge your batteries. Spend most of your job search time networking, by conducting individual meetings and attending social and professional networking events.

## 6\. Create a list of target companies

This will help you create a focus to your job search and assure you are a fit with the organization and position. Always follow up after you submit your resume and after your interview.

## 7\. Celebrate your Success!

![photo-Street](/images/photo-street.jpg)

Undoubtedly you have worked hard to make this important transition.  Share your success and new contact information with your network.  Find a way to celebrate this new chapter in your life.
