**Portland Career Center** is a collective of experienced career counselors and coaches in private practice in Portland, Oregon. Together we bring well over a hundred combined years of helping adults find work that is meaningful, sustainable, and a genuine fit.

## Our mission

We're committed to the art of revealing and developing your relationship to work — today and across a lifetime. Each of us brings our own methods and specialties, but we share one belief: a satisfying career is built from the inside out. Before the résumé and the job search comes the deeper work of understanding your interests, skills, values, and the kind of life you want your work to support.

## How we can help

Whatever stage you're in, there's a good chance one of us has guided someone through it:

- **Career transition** — changing fields, recovering from a layoff, or figuring out what's next
- **Job search** — résumés, interviews, networking, and staying motivated through a hard stretch
- **Growing in your current role** — advancing, navigating a difficult workplace, or deciding when it's time to move on
- **Self-employment** — testing whether working for yourself is the right path
- **Meaning and direction** — reconnecting with what you actually want from your working life

Many of us hold psychologically-oriented graduate degrees — Master's degrees in Counseling, Education, or Training & Development — and several are licensed (LPC) and able to help with the personal side of career change, not just the mechanics. Others come from a coaching tradition focused on momentum, goals, and accountability. We work with clients in our Portland offices, and several of us offer counseling by phone or video for those outside the area.

## A warm place to return to

Career change can feel like climbing a mountain — inspiring from a distance, daunting once you start. Doubt and fatigue are a normal part of the journey, not a sign you're doing it wrong. Think of us as well-trained guides who've walked the route many times: a place to find rest, perspective, and company when you need it.

To get to know each of us — our similarities, our differences, and our specialties — visit [Our Team](/bios), and read the [articles](/blog) we've written. When you're ready to find the right fit, our guide to [choosing a career counselor or coach](/choosing-a-counselor) walks you through it.
